Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 12 November 2026

Welcome to BoxCricketBooking. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details such as mobile number and email address.
  • Account registration and login information.
  • Booking details such as ground, date, time slot and booking status.
  • Payment transaction references and payment status.
  • Ground owner details and ground-related information.
  • Customer support messages and enquiries.

2. Location Information

Users can search for cricket grounds by manually entering a city, area or PIN code.

We may also offer an optional "Use My Current Location" feature. If this feature is available, your device will request permission before sharing location information.

  • GPS access is optional.
  • Users can search using manual location instead.
  • We do not intend to track users continuously in the background.
  • Location information is used to help find nearby grounds.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display available cricket grounds.
  • Process and manage bookings.
  • Manage owner subscriptions.
  • Process and verify payments.
  • Send booking, payment and account-related notifications.
  • Respond to customer support enquiries.
  • Improve platform security and functionality.

4. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. Payment processing is subject to the provider's terms and privacy policy.

We may receive transaction references, payment status and related information required to manage bookings and subscriptions.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared when necessary with:

  • Ground owners, to manage relevant customer bookings.
  • Payment service providers, to process transactions.
  • Technology and hosting service providers, to operate the platform.
  • Government authorities, where disclosure is legally required.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information to advertisers.

6. Data Security

We will take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain booking and payment records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

8. User Rights and Requests

Users may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and necessary transaction records.

9. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. Users should provide accurate information when creating accounts.

10. Third-Party Services

Our platform may use third-party services for payments, hosting, maps or other functionality. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.
