8. Booking Cancellation and Refunds

The following is our proposed cancellation structure, subject to final confirmation and publication:

  • Cancellation 24 hours or more before the booking: 100% refund of the booking amount.

  • If the ground owner cancels a confirmed booking: 100% refund of the booking amount.
  • If a confirmed booking cannot be provided due to double booking: 100% refund of the booking amount.
